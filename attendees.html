Event Format, Benefits & Pass Options

Meridian Exchange is structured as a closed-door roundtable summit conducted over a three-course executive luncheon. Each course is linked to a dedicated policy and infrastructure discussion, allowing participants to engage in meaningful dialogue while contributing to practical recommendations and future implementation frameworks.

A Working Forum, Not a Traditional Conference

Meridian Exchange is designed for moderated strategic discussion, not passive conference attendance. The format combines executive coffee, sponsor briefings, three-course working luncheon roundtables, a coffee roundtable and closing synthesis.

09:30 Arrival
10:00 Welcome Coffee
11:00 Opening Remarks
11:45 First Course Roundtable
12:30 Second Course Roundtable
