About Meridian Exchange

Meridian Exchange is a closed-door policy and industry forum convening senior government, regulatory, infrastructure, cloud, cybersecurity, financial services and technology leaders.

The forum is designed to examine how sovereign jurisdictions, allied economies and critical sectors can strengthen digital sovereignty, infrastructure resilience and continuity frameworks during periods of geopolitical disruption, conflict risk and systemic technology dependence.

Unlike a traditional conference, Meridian Exchange operates as a structured roundtable summit, with working discussions conducted under Chatham House Rule and designed to contribute to practical recommendations, policy pathways and implementation frameworks.

The Four Strategic Roundtables

01

The Legal Lacuna

Data Sovereignty Law in Disruption Conditions

Focus: Examining how data sovereignty rules, service continuity obligations and emergency procedures should work when critical data infrastructure faces severe disruption.

OUTPUT: Legal & Policy Framework
02

The Data Embassy Blueprint

Sovereign Data Continuity Across Borders

Focus: Reviewing how Data Embassy models can help governments and critical sectors maintain sovereign control over essential data, systems and services when infrastructure is hosted across borders under agreed bilateral frameworks.

OUTPUT: Data Embassy Governance Model
03

Conflict-Resilient Infrastructure Architecture

Building a Sovereign Data Node

Focus: Designing sovereign data environments capable of maintaining operations despite geopolitical disruption, cyber incidents, connectivity pressure or infrastructure degradation.

OUTPUT: Resilient Infrastructure Blueprint
04

Quantum Trust Architecture for Sovereign Enclaves

Cross-Border Quantum Security Frameworks

Focus: Exploring PQC, QKD and governance frameworks required to secure sovereign data corridors, Data Embassy environments and cross-border enclave infrastructure.

OUTPUT: Quantum Security Roadmap

Insights & Perspectives

Strategic Issues Shaping Digital Resilience

Exploring the themes behind Meridian Exchange: digital sovereignty, infrastructure resilience and critical service continuity.

01

Why Digital Sovereignty Matters More Than Ever

As digital transformation accelerates, resilience is becoming a strategic imperative for governments and enterprises alike.

Digital sovereignty is increasingly about maintaining control, continuity and trust across critical digital infrastructure.

02

The Hidden Risk of Cloud Concentration

Cloud infrastructure powers modern economies, but growing dependence on a limited number of providers is creating new resilience challenges.

Organisations must balance innovation and efficiency with continuity, redundancy and operational resilience.

03

Infrastructure Resilience Beyond Cybersecurity

Resilience extends beyond cyber defence to include connectivity, infrastructure, supply chains and operational continuity.

The ability to maintain essential services during disruption is becoming a defining capability for modern organisations.
